Library Governance & Code of Conduct

To preserve a quiet, dignified environment for learning and research, all readers and visitors must rigorously adhere to these regulations.

General Code of Conduct

01

Identification & Entry Control

All students, faculty, and visiting scholars must scan their valid Maduka University Smart ID Card at the electronic turnstiles upon entering and exiting the library building. Transferred or borrowed IDs are strictly confiscated.

02

Noise & Silent Study Zones

Strict silence must be observed in all Reading Halls. Mobile phones must be switched to silent mode. Voice calls, music playback without headphones, or group discussions outside designated Collaborative Rooms are prohibited.

03

Food, Beverages & Contraband

Eating, drinking open beverages, smoking, and bringing hazardous materials into reading rooms are strictly banned. Only clear bottled water with secure caps is permitted inside study carrels.

04

Protection of Library Property

Defacing, underlining, tearing, or removing pages from books is treated as acts of vandalism. Offenders will be fined the full replacement cost of the book plus administrative fees and referred to the Disciplinary Committee.
