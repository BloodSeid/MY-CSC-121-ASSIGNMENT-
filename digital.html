Off-Campus Access Remote Instructions

Step-by-Step Login Instructions

  1. Navigate directly to mue.cblibrary.ng in any modern browser.
  2. Click on the "Institutional Student Login" button located at the upper right corner.
  3. Enter your official Maduka Student Email (e.g., john.doe@madukauniversity.edu.ng).
  4. Input your default portal password (your initial Matriculation Number or default PIN issued at registration).
  5. Once authenticated, search through any listed database with seamless remote access privileges!

Digital Lab Rules & Workstation Guidelines

  • System Usage: Lab PCs are reserved strictly for academic research, assignment typing, and database browsing.
  • Storage Security: Save all your work to external USB drives or cloud storage (Google Drive/OneDrive). Local files are wiped daily.
  • Software Restrictions: Installing unauthorized software, playing video games, or modifying system settings is forbidden.
  • Printing Services: Wireless printing to library printers is billed at ₦50 per page at the digital desk.
